# Cross-Team Handoff Checklist

## Business/Product → Platform
- Approved product master and variant master
- SKU and barcode decisions
- Price, cost and margin approvals
- Size chart and measurement method
- Fibre composition, origin and care data
- Product photos and usage rights
- Inventory and launch quantities
- Shipping countries, service levels and return policy
- Legal entity, merchant, importer and invoice details
- Brand tokens, copy, campaign tracking plan and launch calendar

## Platform → Business/Product
- Data templates and validation report
- Staging storefront and mobile preview
- Admin roles and operating instructions
- Product import error report
- Analytics event dictionary and campaign URL rules
- Order/return workflow training
- Release calendar and freeze dates
- Incident and support escalation path
