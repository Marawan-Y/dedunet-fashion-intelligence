## Mission

Prepare an evidence-backed premium fashion business manufactured and packaged in Egypt for a controlled launch into one European market. The first proof is one real product traced from source evidence and physical approval through catalog, web journey, order, shipment, return/refund and reconciliation. No document or PoC substitutes for external reality.

## Accountability

- Side A owns market, customer, product, brand, factory, material/claim, quality, price, policy, fulfilment and operational truth.
- Side B owns architecture, schemas, APIs, software state, security, tests, build, deployment preparation and runtime truth.
- The controller owns cross-side reconciliation, shared registers, handoff routing and gate recommendations. It is not a third delivery side.
- Human/external authorities remain required for founder/IP authority, physical product approval, research participation, legal/tax/customs/product compliance conclusions, registrations, contracts, provider activation and real operations.

## Approved planning boundary

The reversible planning hypothesis is `VS-TEE-001`: one black oversized crew-neck T-shirt in S/M/L, Germany-first, responsive web only. It has zero sellable stock, no approved public brand/product copy, no price, no fibre/origin/quality claim, no final media and no checkout. Germany, product construction, sizes and fulfilment model remain `DRAFT` hypotheses, not launch approval.

Mobile, generative AI, broad catalog, microservices, Kubernetes, public deployment and provider selection are outside the first milestone.

## Evidence and activation rules

Only `DRAFT`, `SELF-VALIDATED`, `AUTOMATED-TESTED`, `HUMAN-VERIFIED`, `EXTERNALLY-VERIFIED`, `BLOCKED`, and `REJECTED` may be used as readiness statuses. A product record begins as `fixture` or `candidate`; it cannot become `sellable` unless all required product, price, market, batch/QC, inventory, content/rights, policy and operator evidence gates pass.

The current PoC is a non-public technical fixture. Its names, descriptions, prices, positive stock, `100% cotton`, Egypt-origin assertions, placeholder images, shipping thresholds and stylist output are rejected as business or market evidence.

## Current gate decision

G0 knowledge-work mobilization is complete. G0 exit is `BLOCKED` and the recommendation is `NO_GO` because founder/IP/company authority, named accountable humans, account ownership and spending thresholds are absent. Reversible G1 preparation may proceed without implying gate advancement.

## Change control

Material changes to market, product, price, customer promise, merchant/importer model, data ownership, security posture, fulfilment or architecture require a new decision-log record and versioned handoff. No side may silently repair the other side's truth.

## Remaining risk and next action
