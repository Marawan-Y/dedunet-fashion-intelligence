## EXT-01 — Governance, founder/IP and authority

Owner: human executive sponsor and qualified counsel, both unassigned. Action required: appoint named accountable humans for every critical role, execute the founders/shareholders or services agreement plus IP-assignment and confidentiality documents, and approve the decision-rights, account-custody and spending-authority matrices. Inputs: participant identities, intended relationship, contributions, funding, IP/assets/accounts, proposed entity, spend/risk thresholds. Procedure: counsel-led structure review; document ownership, IP assignment/confidentiality, roles, exits, approvals, account custody and signatures; execute documents; record named authorities. Questions: who owns pre-existing and new IP; who can bind the venture; who controls domains/repositories/providers; what spend/refund/inventory thresholds apply; what happens on exit/deadlock? Acceptance: executed dated documents; all parties identified; legal review scope recorded; every critical role named; spend/account matrices approved; secure evidence retained. Evidence template: issuer/parties, document ID/version, signed date, review scope, storage URI, verifier, status, limitations. Impact if delayed: DEP-A-001/DEP-001 remain BLOCKED and G0 cannot exit; `operations.roles`, `operations.approval_limits`, `operations.support_hours` and `operations.severity_contacts` stay null so Side B cannot design RBAC, admin authorization or escalation; no material spend, production commitment or production provider account is safe; every later gate inherits an unowned authority gap. Fallback: no material spend, production commitment or production account; keep work to reversible documentation.
