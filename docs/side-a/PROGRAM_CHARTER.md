## Mission

Launch a premium fashion proposition manufactured and packaged in Egypt for one European market only after one real product is traceable from approved physical/source evidence through catalog, order and post-order operations.

## First proof boundary

The first proof is the provisional `VS-TEE-001` black oversized crew-neck T-shirt, sizes S/M/L, for a Germany-first web experience. Fibre, origin, construction, measurements, price, stock, claims and supplier are unknown until evidence arrives. The PoC values are synthetic fixtures, not business truth.

## Ownership

Side A owns customer, product, claim, price, factory, quality, policy, fulfilment and operating truth. Side B owns schemas, APIs, runtime and security truth. The controller owns reconciled shared files. Human executive, legal, finance, product-approval and external-specialist names remain unassigned and block G0 exit.

## Evidence and change control

Only the seven repository statuses are permitted. External actions are never inferred from document preparation. Every business field carries owner, verification state and evidence reference. Changes to market, product, price, customer promise, importer, claims or fulfilment require a versioned change request and formal handoff.

## Gate objective

G0 establishes workspace, registers and ownership, but exits only after the founder/IP baseline, named accountable owners and spending authority exist. No calendar target overrides failure.
