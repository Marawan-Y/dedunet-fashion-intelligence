## 1. Meaning and historical background

**DEDUNET** is treated as a **contemporary constructed brand spelling**, not as a historically verified ancient name.

The reliable historical reference is **Dedun/Dedwen (Ddwn)**, a Nubian deity documented in Egyptian sources. Current scholarly and source-based material associates Dedun/Dedwen with Nubia, incense and valuable resources moving north through Nile-region exchange. The Pyramid Texts refer to Dedwen in connection with clean incense, and modern Egyptological scholarship identifies the name as likely Nubian in origin.

The project did **not** find authoritative Egyptological support for the popular online claim that “Dedunet” was an established goddess of textiles and weaving. That claim appears in low-authority web material and must not be repeated as fact without an Egyptologist's written validation.

**Historical-use rule:**

> DEDUNET may say its name is a modern construction inspired by Dedun/Dedwen and ideas of material value, movement and exchange. It must not claim to be the historically correct name of an ancient weaving goddess.

Reference sources:

- UCLA Encyclopedia of Egyptology material discussing Dedwen as a likely Nubian theonym: https://escholarship.org/content/qt2fb8t2pz/qt2fb8t2pz.pdf
- James P. Allen, *The Ancient Egyptian Pyramid Texts*, including Dedwen and incense: https://www.sbl-site.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/AncientEgyptianPyramid_Allen_SBL.pdf
- Routledge reference work on Egyptian deities: https://www.routledge.com/The-Routledge-Dictionary-of-Egyptian-Gods-and-Goddesses/Hart/p/book/9780415344951

## 2. Modern interpretation

DEDUNET translates the historical association with valuable material and exchange into a contemporary fashion principle:

> Value is not decoration. Value is what material, form and use carry together.

Fashion is the modern medium because clothing physically carries material, identity, memory and movement. DEDUNET creates unisex pieces whose worth is expressed through proportion, construction, versatility and transparent product information rather than loud status symbolism.

**Strategic formula:**

> DEDUNET = a modern name inspired by material value and passage.  
> Fashion = material carried on the body and shaped by use.  
> Brand role = turn substance into contemporary form.

## 5. Brand promise

**Form with substance.**

DEDUNET promises clear design, useful versatility and evidence-safe product information. Material quality, origin, durability and environmental performance may only be claimed when documented.

## 6. Mission

To create modern garments and objects whose value comes from considered form, cultural intelligence, useful wear and transparent product truth.

## 7. Vision

To build a globally relevant North African-origin lifestyle brand recognised for contemporary design, respectful cultural translation and products that hold meaning through use.

## 11. Cultural boundaries

### Allowed

- Explicit credit that Dedun/Dedwen is Nubian in origin and appears in Egyptian sources.
- Nile-region material and trade history discussed with sources.
- Egyptian and Nubian contemporary creative references developed with relevant collaborators.
- Sandstone, resin, smoke, indigo, clay and architectural rhythm as abstract visual cues.
- Arabic/Latin typography used with linguistic accuracy.

### Prohibited without specialist approval

- Calling Dedunet a verified ancient Egyptian weaving goddess.
- Erasing Nubian origin by presenting the reference as exclusively Egyptian.
- Commercial depictions of Dedun/Dedwen as a mascot or chatbot.
- Random hieroglyphics, pharaoh masks, pyramids, scarabs or ankhs.
- Incense, temple or funerary imagery used as decorative exoticism.
- Claims of an uninterrupted ancient craft lineage.
- Sacred texts or symbols on footwear, seating surfaces or disposable packaging.

## 12. Claims requiring proof

The following remain blocked until supporting evidence is attached:

- “100% Egyptian cotton” or any Egyptian-cotton claim.
- Cotton variety, fibre origin or geographic-origin claims.
- Made in Egypt / manufactured in Egypt for any live SKU.
- Organic, recycled, sustainable, eco-friendly, low-impact or carbon claims.
- Ethical, fair-wage, responsibly made or artisan-made claims.
- Certified materials or factories.
- Premium quality, superior durability or long-life performance as factual claims.
- Laboratory-tested colourfastness, shrinkage, pilling or strength.
- Handmade or traditional-craft claims.
- Any statement that DEDUNET is an authenticated ancient name or textile deity.

**Safe prototype wording:** “Intended specification; supplier verification required.”
