# DEDUNET Open Risks and External Actions

## Blocking before public launch

1. Obtain an EU/German professional trademark and company-name opinion, especially against DeDeNet GmbH.
2. Search Classes 25, 35, 18, 24, 9 and 42, including DEDENET/DeDeNet and DEDUN/Dedwen variants.
3. Ask an Egyptologist or Nubian-studies specialist to review all historical name language.
4. Test pronunciation and Arabic rendering with target customers.
5. Confirm legal entity, EU importer/responsible operator, privacy, returns, delivery and imprint information.
6. Validate factory, material, origin, care and quality claims with documents, samples and tests.
7. Redraw and visually search the final logo before filing or bulk production.
8. Confirm domains and social handles directly; search-engine absence is not availability.

## Non-blocking for internal platform prototype

- Seed the five fictional products with zero stock.
- Use concept SVGs with visible prototype disclaimers.
- Implement DEDUNET Guide only as an internal UI label.
- Use the exact tokens, navigation and content JSON supplied.

## Hard truth

The brand name carries high Germany/app risk because **DeDeNet** is already an active German software company with mobile products. The prototype can proceed; public commercial commitment should not.
