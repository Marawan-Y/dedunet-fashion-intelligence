## About page

**Headline:** Material carries more than weight.

DEDUNET begins with a question: what gives an object real worth?

The name is a contemporary construction inspired by Dedun or Dedwen, a Nubian deity documented in Egyptian sources and associated with incense and valuable resources moving through the Nile region. The historical reference is credited carefully. DEDUNET does not claim to be an ancient textile-goddess name, and it does not reproduce religious imagery as fashion decoration.

Our modern interpretation is material worth. A garment becomes valuable through the way material, proportion, construction and use meet. That value is quiet. It appears in how a shirt layers, how a trouser moves, how a surface changes with wear and how clearly the product is described.

The brand is Egyptian-origin in its business ambition and North African in its cultural field, while recognising the Nubian source behind the name. Our work must be developed through contemporary collaboration, accurate attribution and product evidence—not mythology cosplay or borrowed sacred symbols.

First Passage is the prototype expression of that idea: five connected pieces designed as a controlled unisex wardrobe. Materials, origin and manufacturing remain intended specifications until supplier documents, samples and testing confirm them.
