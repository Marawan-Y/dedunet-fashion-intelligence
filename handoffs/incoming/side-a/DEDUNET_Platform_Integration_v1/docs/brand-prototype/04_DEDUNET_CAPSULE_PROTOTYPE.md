## Capsule logic

**First Passage** is a five-piece unisex wardrobe. The products share clean vertical lines, relaxed proportion, controlled layering and one restrained passage detail. The capsule is intentionally small enough for the platform team to seed, filter, recommend and test without inventing missing records.
