## Executive risk conclusion

**DEDUNET status: `HIGH PRELIMINARY RISK — PROFESSIONAL CLEARANCE REQUIRED`**

DEDUNET remains usable for the internal fictional prototype. It is **not approved for public launch, trademark filing, packaging production, paid media, domain-led rollout or app-store release** until a qualified EU/German trademark professional completes an identical and similarity search.

The principal risk is not a direct fashion brand found in this preliminary scan. The principal risk is the active German company **DeDeNet GmbH**, whose name differs by only one vowel and whose business includes software, mobile solutions, retail-facing services and published mobile apps. That creates serious visual, phonetic, company-name, digital-service and app-store risk for a Germany-first launch.

A second discoverability problem is the published technical term **DEDUNet**, used for an image-enhancement neural-network architecture. This is not automatically a trademark conflict, but it weakens software/app search ownership.

## Trademark versus copyright

- Brand names, taglines and source identifiers are primarily **trademark** matters.
- Original logos, illustrations, photos, patterns and writing may involve **copyright**.
- Independent creation does not prevent a new logo from infringing a prior trademark.
- Ancient public-domain references do not make modern third-party illustrations free to copy.
- Final marks require professional redrawing and visual-similarity searching.
