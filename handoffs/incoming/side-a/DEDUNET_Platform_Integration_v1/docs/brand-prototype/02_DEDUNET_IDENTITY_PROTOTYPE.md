# DEDUNET Complete Identity Prototype

**Artifact ID:** A3-DEDUNET-IDENTITY-v1.0  
**Status:** SELF-VALIDATED — CONCEPT LOGOS, NOT FILING-READY

## Identity idea: Material Worth

DEDUNET's identity treats value as something carried by material and form rather than displayed through ornamental luxury. The visual system combines high-contrast editorial typography with one repeated device: a passage line interrupted by a resin-coloured source point.

## Logo system

### Primary wordmark

- Uppercase **DEDUNET** with controlled spacing and a high-contrast editorial character.
- A horizontal passage line sits beneath the wordmark.
- One Resin source point marks the transition from origin to use.
- Use for website headers, packaging fronts, invoices, campaigns and email mastheads.
- Minimum digital width: 160 px.
- Minimum print width: 35 mm.

### Compact mark

- A simplified **D/N passage monogram**.
- The left form suggests a contained material source; the diagonal suggests movement; the final vertical establishes destination and structure.
- A Resin point marks connection, not decoration.
- Use for garment embroidery, labels, social avatar, app launcher and compact navigation.
- Minimum digital size: 24 px; use the simplified favicon at 16 px.
- Minimum embroidery height: 14 mm after stitch testing.

### Monochrome and reversed marks

- Monochrome: solid Carbon or black.
- Reversed: Sandstone or white mark on Carbon.
- No gradients, metallic simulations, shadows or outlines in the core logo system.

### App icon and favicon

- Carbon square field.
- Sandstone D/N monogram.
- Resin source point.
- App icon: 1024×1024 PNG plus SVG source.
- Favicon: simplified SVG with no tagline or thin editorial text.

### Logo restrictions

- Do not add a deity, incense burner, pyramid, scarab or hieroglyph to the mark.
- Do not use the compact mark as the only brand identifier at first customer contact.
- Do not alter the Resin point position.
- Do not compress or expand the wordmark.
- Do not place the wordmark on uncontrolled busy photography.
- Do not imitate DeDeNet's casing, software-product structure or visual identity.

- Maximum content width: 1280 px.

- 12-column desktop grid / 4-column mobile grid.
- Button radius: 0 px.
- Card radius: 0 px.
- Input radius: 0 px.
- Border: 1 px, low contrast.
- Asymmetric editorial compositions are allowed; commerce grids remain disciplined and predictable.
- Empty space is functional and must not be filled with pseudo-historical ornament.

## Pattern and motif

**Passage Lines**

- Parallel lines interrupted by one or two small Resin source points.
- Suggests material movement, seam paths and exchange without mapping an actual historic route.
- Pattern opacity: 5–10%.
- Use on tissue, email banners, app empty states and editorial dividers.
- Never place across product details or legibility-critical UI.

## Photography direction

- Quiet editorial realism.
- Mixed-gender and gender-fluid styling.
- Direct posture, natural movement and controlled expression.
- Materials: plaster, stone, canvas, timber, oxidised metal and urban shadow.
- Lighting: directional side light with soft falloff; low saturation.
- Cultural context through people, place, material and credited collaboration—not costume or props.
- Avoid temples, pyramids, staged incense smoke, “desert mysticism,” excessive gold and generic safari colour grading.

## Product-image ratios

- Product listing and PDP primary: 4:5.
- Product detail crop: 1:1.
- Homepage campaign: 16:9 desktop / 4:5 mobile art direction.
- Collection cover: 3:2 desktop / 4:5 mobile.
- Social square: 1:1.
- App splash/social story: 9:16.
- Email banner: 3:1.

## Production status

The included SVGs are implementation-ready **prototype vectors**, not filing-ready or bulk-production masters. Before launch, a professional designer must refine optical spacing, convert approved type to outlines, conduct a visual trademark search and test embroidery, screen print, heat transfer and woven-label reproduction.
