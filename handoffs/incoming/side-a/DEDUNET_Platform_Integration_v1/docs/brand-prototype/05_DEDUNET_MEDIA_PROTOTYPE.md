# DEDUNET Media Prototype

**Artifact ID:** A7-DEDUNET-MEDIA-v1.0  
**Status:** SELF-VALIDATED — CONCEPT MEDIA ONLY

## Required platform media delivered

- One homepage hero.
- One collection cover.
- Front, back and detail concept assets for all five products.
- One lifestyle concept for each of the three hero products.
- One About-page concept.
- One social avatar.
- One app splash.
- One email banner.

## Direction

The concept system uses Carbon, Sandstone, Resin and Indigo geometry, passage lines and controlled negative space. It deliberately avoids temples, pyramids, deity depictions and staged incense imagery.

## Production rule

All supplied media is labelled **concept image**. It is not photography of a manufactured product and must not be used to imply that samples, materials, factories or finished garments exist.
